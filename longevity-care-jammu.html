Longevity and Preventive Care in Jammu

Longevity care is preventive medicine with a long view: finding the risks to your health early and keeping them under control for years. Dr. Anil Kumar Dhar, an internal medicine physician (MBBS, MD, DNB) with more than 30 years of practice, provides it at his clinic on Canal Road, Bhagwati Nagar, Jammu.

What longevity care means at this clinic

The clinic's longevity care focuses on healthy ageing, metabolic health, nutritional guidance and stress management. It is not one treatment. It is a way of looking after an adult over many years, so that the common long-term conditions are prevented where they can be, found early where they cannot, and kept under control once they are found.

No doctor can promise a longer life. What medicine can do is lower the known risks, and that is the aim.

The conditions that matter most

Some of the largest risks to an adult's health build up without symptoms: raised blood pressure, raised blood sugar and raised cholesterol. Each can be measured, and each can be treated. The clinic provides blood pressure and heart care, diabetes care and thyroid treatment, and one physician looks at them together.

Where to start

A baseline comes first. The clinic's general check-up costs ₹1,000; the general physician page lists what it covers and what to bring. From the results, the doctor advises what to change, what to treat and when to come back.

Food, activity, sleep and stress

Medicines are one part of the plan. What you eat, how much you move, how you sleep and how you handle stress all affect blood pressure, sugar and weight. The clinic's longevity care includes nutritional guidance and stress management, with advice fitted to you.

Vaccines for adults

Preventing infections is part of ageing well, more so for older adults and for people with diabetes, heart disease or lung disease. The clinic offers adult vaccination, including the annual flu vaccine and the pneumonia vaccine.

Frequently asked questions

Who is longevity care for?

Any adult who wants to look after their long-term health, with or without an existing condition. It is useful if you have a family history of diabetes or heart disease, or have not had a check-up in a while.

Is longevity care a separate treatment?

No. It is preventive care by an internal medicine physician: check-ups, control of blood pressure, sugar and cholesterol, advice on food and stress, and vaccination.
