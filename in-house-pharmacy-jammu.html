In-house Pharmacy at the Clinic in Jammu

Dr. Anil Kumar Dhar's clinic on Canal Road, Bhagwati Nagar, Jammu, has its own pharmacy. The medicines the doctor prescribes are available immediately after your consultation, which saves an extra trip to an outside chemist.

WhatsApp to Book Call Clinic

How it works

You see the doctor, and the pharmacy at the clinic dispenses the medicines on your prescription before you leave. If anything about a medicine is not clear, such as when to take it or for how long, you can ask at the clinic while you are still there.

Why it helps

You do not have to look for the medicines elsewhere after the visit. That helps patients who find a second stop difficult: older adults, people who are unwell, and people who have travelled to reach the clinic.

Long-term medicines

Patients with diabetes, blood pressure or thyroid conditions often take medicines for years, and the dose can change at a follow-up visit. Bring the medicines you take now, or their strips, so that the doctor can check them against the new prescription.

Tests at the clinic

The clinic also offers a lab collection service, so a blood sample can be given at the clinic when the doctor asks for a test.

Paying

The clinic accepts cash and all UPI apps.
